AODV-Inspired Low-Overhead Routing for Underwater Drone Swarms Using Acoustic Communication

Research Overview

This project focuses on studying and reproducing a routing approach designed to reduce communication overhead in underwater drone swarms using acoustic communication.

Underwater acoustic networks face significant challenges, including limited bandwidth, long propagation delays, high bit error rates, and energy constraints. These limitations make efficient routing an important research problem, particularly in mobile underwater networks where changes in connectivity may require route recovery.

The project aims to analyze the proposed approach, understand its underlying design principles, and investigate its performance based on the methodology and experimental results reported in the reference paper.

Research Objectives

Understand the challenges of routing in underwater acoustic networks.

Analyze the proposed approach to reducing routing overhead.

Study the methodology and implementation described in the reference paper.

Reproduce the proposed approach and evaluate its performance against the reported results.
